## Requirements

- The machine must stay awake for the whole sweep: sleep and hibernation disabled (Windows: `powercfg /change standby-timeout-ac 0` and `powercfg /hibernate off`).
- The GPU must be free of other workloads; the benchmarks in `src/bench.py` additionally require that no training job is running.
- The queue runner resumes after interruptions: re-run the same `make` target.
